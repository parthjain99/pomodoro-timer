Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What is stored

Pomodoro saves your tasks, projects, timer settings, and list of YouTube videos in cookies on your device. The cookie that records your choice on the cookie notice is also stored on your device.

What is not collected

Pomodoro has no accounts and no sign-up. Your data is not sent to our servers, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

YouTube

Videos play through YouTube's embedded player. YouTube, and Google as its owner, may set their own cookies and collect data when you play a video. Their policies apply to that data: Google privacy policy.

Your choices

You can clear the cookies in your browser at any time, which resets Pomodoro to its default state on that browser. The desktop app stores its data on your computer and is not affected by browser cookies.
