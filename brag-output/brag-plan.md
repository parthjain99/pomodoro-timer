# Brag Plan: Pomodoro

## What is this app?
A desktop Pomodoro timer with tasks, projects, analytics, and an optional YouTube player that follows the timer (from the project README).

## The angle
The app's own sentence is the joke: the player follows the timer. The video shows that exact behavior with the real UI, not a marketing pitch.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Text on a dark card: "The player follows the timer." (README wording)

## Key moments (the middle)
- Timer card: "Focus / Short Break / Long Break" tabs and the 25:00 clock starting to count down.
- Task list: tasks appear one by one, and the current task is marked "Working".
- Focus Music card: pressing play in the video starts the timer.

## Outro / punchline
"Pomodoro" wordmark with the logo. Final line: "A desktop Pomodoro timer. The player follows the timer."

## User flow worth showing
Entry: open the app (timer at 25:00) -> key action: add a task and press play in the video -> result: the timer runs and the task reads "Working".

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: playful, clean, postable; no Halloween theme (the logo carries that)
- Interpretation: short holds, fast motion, and lines lifted directly from the app.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 22s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f5f5f5 (page), #ffffff (cards); dark hook card #111111
- Accent: #ff6b81 (primary buttons, active tab); violet #4f3bd6 (selected states)
- Text: #111111 (primary), #6b6b70 (muted)
- Display font: system sans (-apple-system, Segoe UI), bold
- Body font: system sans
- Clock font: monospace (ui-monospace, SF Mono, Menlo)
- Strongest visual element: the big monospace clock with the rounded "Focus" tab

## Share copy (draft)
Made Pomodoro, a desktop timer where the YouTube player follows the timer. Press play in the video and your focus session starts.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120 BPM), starts at 0s, fades out under the logo
- Music treatment: volume 0.35, fade-out last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: strong cues at 16.02s and 17.02s (Scene 4 payoff, "timer starts"); beat grid from 3.02s every 0.5s
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (keep the scene readable)
- SFX posture: sparse; keyboard tick on the hook line only; card sound on task rows
- Audio-coupled moments: task rows (card place) on beat grid; timer start on strong cue 16.02s
- Restraint rule: no sound during the analytics line

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 - Hook - 2.5s
Dark card, centered text "The player follows the timer." appears (0.4s), holds to the end.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: quiet, confident open
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: bed starts at 0s
Transition mood: hard cut -> Scene 2

### Scene 2 - The timer - 4s
Light app window. Tabs "Focus / Short Break / Long Break" slide in. Clock shows 25:00 and starts counting down (24:58, 24:57...). Hold 1.2s on the counting clock.
Sequential/interaction: yes - tabs appear one by one
Audio intent: the app comes alive
Audio-coupled idea: tabs pop on beats 3.02, 3.52, 4.02
Music: bed
Transition mood: clean -> Scene 3

### Scene 3 - Tasks - 5s
"Add a new task" row, then three task rows appear one by one: "Write chapter 2", "Email advisor", "Thesis" (project chip). The first row gets the "Working" badge and tinted highlight. Rows are text, so each holds 0.8s settled.
Sequential/interaction: yes - rows arrive every other beat
Audio intent: card sounds
Audio-coupled idea: rows on beat grid 6.52, 7.52, 8.52
Music: bed
Transition mood: clean -> Scene 4

### Scene 4 - Video starts the timer - 5s
Focus Music card: pasted link, Load button; a player frame with a pulsing play overlay; press play, then the clock switches to "Pause" and the timer counts. Caption: "Press play in the video. The timer starts."
Sequential/interaction: simulated click on play
Audio intent: payoff
Audio-coupled idea: timer start lands on strong cue 16.02s
Music: bed, fades down slightly
Transition mood: soft -> Scene 5

### Scene 5 - Analytics - 2s
Analytics card: "Focus time", "Sessions", and an "Export CSV" button. Caption: "Every session, exported as CSV." Holds 1.2s.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: quiet, no SFX
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: bed (no SFX)
Transition mood: clean -> Scene 6

### Scene 6 - Outro - 3.5s
Logo fades in with the "Pomodoro" wordmark. Final line: "A desktop Pomodoro timer. The player follows the timer." Music fades out over the last 1.5s.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: resolve
Audio-coupled idea: music out
Music: fades out
Transition mood: soft

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat
**Audio summary:** a warm bed from 0s, card sounds on the task rows, the timer-start payoff on the strong cue at 16s, then a fade under the logo.
